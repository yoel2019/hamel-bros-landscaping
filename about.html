Our story

A Metro West landscaping business established in 2001.

Hamel Bros. Landscaping was established in 2001 by Paul and Dave Hamel. From the beginning, customer service, accessibility and quality work have been treated as equally important to the work itself.

The company serves commercial and residential customers and maintains a well-trained, dedicated workforce able to complete projects of any size. For most landscaping projects, Hamel Bros. serves the surrounding Metro West area.

To remain local and responsive to weekly maintenance customers, lawn care services are limited to Natick and Wellesley.
