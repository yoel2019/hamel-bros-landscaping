More Than Just Landscapers

Hamel Bros. Landscaping was founded in 2001 by brothers Paul and Dave Hamel with a clear mission: deliver professional-grade landscaping while treating every client like a neighbor.

Over two decades later, that mission hasn't changed. We've grown our team, expanded our services, and built lasting relationships throughout the Metro West area — but we've never lost sight of what matters most: your satisfaction.

The Hamel Family

Meet Our Team

Four brothers, one mission. Get to know the people behind every perfectly manicured lawn.

Paul Hamel

Co-Founder & Operations

A 1998 graduate of Bentley University. Paul oversees day-to-day operations, trains staff, works with customers on design projects, and manages pruning and nursery stock purchasing.

"A great yard doesn't just happen — it's carefully planned, expertly executed, and maintained with pride."

Dave Hamel

Co-Founder & Client Relations

A 1998 graduate of Norwich University. Dave handles daily scheduling, estimates, and — most importantly — communicating with clients. You'll often find him out in the field planting trees or operating the backhoe.

"Listening to our clients is the foundation of every great project we've ever delivered."

Steve Hamel

Lawn Care Supervisor

A valued Hamel Bros. employee since 2001. Steve oversees all commercial and residential lawn care operations and is also responsible for fleet and equipment maintenance.

"The best lawn care is the kind you never have to worry about — and that's what we deliver."

Jimmy Hamel

Landscaping Crew Lead

The youngest Hamel brother. Jimmy leads the general landscaping crew, overseeing mulch installation, planting, and pruning. He is a graduate of the University of Buffalo where he was captain of the Division 1 wrestling program.

"We approach every job with the same discipline and teamwork I learned on the mat."
